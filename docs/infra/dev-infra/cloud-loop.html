The cloud loop: close the laptop, keep building

His goal: "close the laptop and continue the work without feeling the difference, and sit back down and open it when I want to." This page follows one change from the phone to ⌘K. Pick a scenario, then step through it.

Why it's shaped like this

ConstraintSo
A closed Mac is asleep and runs nothingCI runs on GitHub's machines, never the Mac
Polling costs battery and does nothing while closedNo timer. The app kicks the CD job on unlock/wake; launchd also runs it at login
The founder version lives on his MacCD runs on the Mac, at the moment it's awake and his
The cloud VM and the CI Mac have no screen he seesThey can test and compile the pill, not show it. Notch/UI work stays at the Mac
His database never leaves the MacCloud sessions can't run evals on his real lines
He may be mid-work when he unlocksautorelease skips unless the repo is a clean main

What a cloud session brings from his setup

The cloud VM starts from a fresh clone. What's committed to the repo comes along; what lives only on his Mac doesn't. (Claude Code docs, "What carries over from your setup".)

ThingIn the cloud?Why
Repo CLAUDE.md (incl. the "Cloud sessions" rules)YesPart of the clone
Repo .claude/settings.json hooks (build loop, learn trigger)YesPart of the clone (one-repo session)
Repo .claude/skills/, agents/, .mcp.jsonYesPart of the clone
Skills enabled on claude.aiYesLoaded from his account
~/.claude/CLAUDE.md (the brain pointer), user skills (layered-docs…), plugins, memoryNoLive on his Mac. To bring one along, commit it to the repo's .claude/
MCP servers added locally (daylog MCP)NoIn ~/.claude.json on the Mac; and the daylog server is on localhost anyway
GitHub credentialsProxiedStay encrypted on Anthropic's servers; a proxy attaches a scoped credential to git operations. No token in the VM
